# Pomegranate ERP Professional

A professional farm management ERP designed for pomegranate cultivation.

## Vision
Digital management system for export-quality pomegranate farming with complete records, planning and analysis.

## Current Farm Profile
- Crop: Pomegranate (Sharad King)
- Plants: 600
- Planting Date: 6-7 June 2026
- Spacing: 12 ft x 7 ft
- Location: Tavashi, Pandharpur, Solapur, Maharashtra

## Modules
- Farm Dashboard
- Single Data Entry Activity Log
- Irrigation Management
- Fertigation Management
- Spray Management
- Disease & Pest Library
- Biological Input Library
- Inventory Management
- Expense Tracking
- Reports & Analytics
