# Expense and Financial Management System Specification

## Purpose
Create complete farm financial tracking connected with all ERP operations.

## Expense Record Fields
- Expense ID
- Date picker
- Category
- Activity/module link
- Product or service
- Company/supplier
- Quantity
- Unit cost
- Total cost
- Payment details
- Notes

## Expense Categories
Supports:
- Spray cost
- Fertilizer cost
- Irrigation cost
- Labour cost
- Equipment cost
- Planting cost
- Maintenance cost
- Other farm expenses

## Automatic Cost Connections

Spray:
- Product cost × quantity used

Fertigation:
- Fertilizer cost × quantity used

Inventory:
- Purchase records update expenses

Harvest:
- Income records connect with production

## Financial Reports
- Daily expenses
- Monthly expenses
- Module-wise cost
- Plot-wise cost
- Total investment
- Income vs expense
- Profit calculation

## CRUD Support
- Add
- View
- Edit
- Delete/Archive
- Search
- Filter

## Future Ready
Supports budget planning, profit forecasting and financial analytics.
