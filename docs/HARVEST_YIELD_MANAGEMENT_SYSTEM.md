# Harvest and Yield Management System Specification

## Purpose
Track pomegranate production, quality, sales and profitability.

## Harvest Record Fields
- Harvest ID
- Date picker
- Plot selection
- Plant age
- Harvest cycle
- Number of fruits
- Total weight
- Average fruit weight
- Fruit quality grade
- Brix value (optional)
- Rejection quantity
- Notes
- Photos

## Quality Management
Track:
- Export quality fruits
- Premium grade
- Local market grade
- Damaged/rejected fruits

## Income Management
Fields:
- Buyer/customer
- Quantity sold
- Price per kg
- Total income
- Payment status

## Connections

Plant Module:
- Compare yield with plant history

Expense Module:
- Calculate production cost

Reports:
- Yield per plot
- Yield per plant
- Income analysis
- Profit calculation

## CRUD Support
- Add
- View
- Edit
- Delete/Archive
- Search
- Filter

## Future Ready
Supports market tracking, export records, quality prediction and yield forecasting.
